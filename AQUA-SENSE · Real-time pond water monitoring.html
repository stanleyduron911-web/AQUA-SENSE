How a service day works

From booking to a clean, re-calibrated unit.

  1. Book & site check — we confirm your pond, date and fee.
  2. Prepare & calibrate — pH buffers, TDS standard, turbidity standards, temperature check.
  3. Verify — compared against a calibrated reference meter.
  4. Set up at your pond — probes in the water; we show you how to join the dashboard.
  5. Monitor all day — a team member stays with the unit and explains any changes.
  6. Review & pack up — we go over the day’s readings with you, then clean and re-calibrate.

What we measure

Temperature

🧪

pH

🔬

TDS

Conductivity-based dissolved solids probe.

🌫️

Turbidity

Infrared probe — best for spotting sudden changes.
